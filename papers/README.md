## Why all three belong here

### Part I

Du Val sets up the class of isolated surface singularities whose presence does not alter the adjunction conditions. The language predates the modern `A_n`, `D_n`, `E_6`, `E_7`, `E_8` package.

### Part II

The publisher's extract states the central geometric pattern particularly clearly: the neighborhood is resolved into connected chains or trees of rational curves of grade `−2`. Curves that meet correspond to reflecting hyperplanes at angle `π/3`; curves that do not meet correspond to perpendicular hyperplanes. This is already the geometry-to-reflection-group bridge behind the later ADE diagrams.

### Part III

Du Val relates configurations of actual rational `(-2)`-curves on rational surfaces to reflection-generated subgroups of the relevant symmetry groups and supplies existence constructions in the cases he studies.

These are only retrieval notes from the publisher records and extracts. They are not a substitute for a close reading of the full text.

## Copyright and storage decision

The Cambridge records state copyright by the Cambridge Philosophical Society. The PDFs have therefore **not** been copied into this repository. This folder puts the papers into the project as exact bibliographic records and stable source links without redistributing the publisher files.

If a lawful public-domain or explicitly licensed scan is later found, add it under `papers/originals/` with:

- provenance;
- checksum;
- source URL;
- license or public-domain basis;
- an unchanged copy of the scan;
- a separate transcription/correction file rather than silently altering the source.

## Attribution trail visible in the papers

Du Val explicitly connects his configurations to the reflection-group work of H. S. M. Coxeter and cites older algebraic-surface sources. Later notes should retain those names rather than turning “Du Val singularity” into a single-author origin story.
